## 🛠️ What I Build For You

</div>

- 🌐 **Business Website**: clean, professional site with your products, services, prices and location
- 🛒 **Online Ordering System**: customers browse, add to cart and place orders, and orders reach you directly
- 📅 **Booking Website**: customers pick a service and book an appointment online
- 📊 **Owner Dashboard**: see and manage orders or bookings in one place
- 🚀 **Hosting & Launch**: I put your site live so you can share the link right away
- 📱 **Mobile First Design**: most customers use phones, so every site is built for small screens first
